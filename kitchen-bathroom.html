Culinary Hygiene

Kitchen Deep Cleaning & Degreasing

Every day cooking leaves fine oil mists that settle onto cabinets, tiles, and appliances, attracting dust and turning sticky over time.

Cleanfox uses food-safe, high-potency alkaline degreasers that safely dissolve heavy oil coatings without harming your expensive acrylic, laminate, or quartz surfaces.

  • Chimney & Baffle Filters: Complete grease removal and steam rinse
  • Stove & Backsplash Tiles: Carbon stain and burnt splatter scrub
  • Kitchen Sink & Faucets: Mineral scale removal & 99.9% bactericidal sanitization
  • Cabinet Exterior & Interior: Non-abrasive microfiber wipe-down
Sanitary Excellence

Bathroom Sanitization & Descaling

Navi Mumbai water supplies frequently leave stubborn white calcium and magnesium scale on glass shower doors, chrome taps, and wall tiles.

Our specialized acid-free descaling agents break the mineral bonds effortlessly, restoring crystal transparency and chrome shine without eroding grout lines.

  • Shower Cubicle Glass: Hard-water etched scale and soap scum descaling
  • Wall & Floor Tile Grout: Deep mechanical brushing to eliminate mold and discoloration
  • Commode & Washbasin: Hospital-grade germicidal sanitation and stain extraction
  • Chrome Fixtures & Taps: Mirror-finish buffing and descaling
Standardized Protocol
