Methodology

Our Scientific 5-Stage Approach

From initial site evaluation to the final quality walk-through, every stage follows a strict protocol.

01

Initial Site Inspection & Assessment

Our team supervisor inspects high-traffic zones, identifying stubborn stains, grease buildup, and delicate surface materials (e.g. Italian marble, solid teak, velvet).

02

Zone Preparation & Dust Extraction

Dry dust, cobwebs, and loose particles are extracted using high-power HEPA industrial vacuums across ceiling trims, light fixtures, switchboards, and sliding window channels.

03

Intensive Chemical & Degreasing Treatment

Specialized eco-safe formulations are applied to dissolve kitchen grease on chimneys, tile grout lines, and bathroom hard-water scale on taps and glass enclosures.

04

Mechanical Machine Scrubbing & Detailing

Floors undergo single-disc rotary scrubbing. Mirrors, windows, and metal fixtures are hand-buffed to a streak-free optical shine.

05

Supervisor Quality Audit & Signoff

The supervisor walks room-by-room with you using our 50-point checklist, ensuring every commitment has been executed to complete satisfaction.
