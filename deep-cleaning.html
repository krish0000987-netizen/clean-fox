Total Scope

What Is Covered in Our Deep Cleaning

A systematic, multi-point treatment for every room and surface inside your residence.

1. High-Reach Dust & Cobwebs

Complete dry-brushing and vacuuming of ceiling corners, false ceilings, cornices, fans, tube lights, and exhaust vents.

2. Windows, Grills & Sliding Tracks

Detailed channel vacuuming, mosquito mesh washing, window glass polishing with rubber squeegees for streak-free transparency.

3. Modular Kitchen Degreasing

Chimney filter and outer body degreasing, stove scrub, tile backsplash, countertop sanitization, and cabinet wipe-down.

4. Bathroom Hard Water Descaling

Shower enclosure glass descaling, ceramic basin scrubbing, WC bowl chemical sanitization, and tap chrome buffing.

5. Doors, Frames & Switchboards

Careful dry and damp microfiber wiping of all electrical plates, wooden door frames, handles, and skirting trims.

6. Machine Floor Scrubbing

Single-disc rotary floor scrubbing to remove accumulated dirt layers from tile grout lines, marble, and vitrified flooring.

Step-by-Step Execution

The Cleanfox Deep Cleaning Process

Our disciplined timeline ensures no detail is overlooked from arrival to handover.

01

Inspection & Assessment

The supervisor inspects the entire residence with you, noting delicate materials, areas requiring extra degreasing, and client focus zones.

02

Preparation & High-Power Dry Extraction

Heavy machinery and eco-friendly solutions are arranged. HEPA vacuums clear all loose dust, ceiling corners, and window channels.

03

Targeted Deep Scrubbing & Degreasing

Kitchen chimneys, tiles, kitchen counters, and bathroom shower areas receive deep chemical reaction treatment to dissolve grime and scale.

04

Machine Floor Scrubbing & Detailing

Floors undergo rotary machine scrubbing and wet extraction. Glass, mirrors, and metallic fittings are hand-polished.

05

Final Supervisor Audit & Quality Check

You review each room together with our lead supervisor to ensure 100% satisfaction before the crew packs up.
